# Typerelay Cloud

Typerelay Cloud is the hosted service for managing reusable text, code and shared snippet libraries without deploying your own server. Choose a plan on the [Typerelay pricing page](https://typerelay.com/pricing/), then follow the [getting started guide](../guide/getting-started) to create your first library and snippet.

## Choose a plan for your workflow

Free is a starting point for personal shortcuts on one machine. Pro adds the connected individual workflow, including web sync and API or MCP access. Team adds shared libraries and team management for people maintaining reusable wording together. Check the pricing page for current prices and the [billing guide](./billing) for account limits, trials, subscription changes and white-label settings.

Choose based on how you intend to maintain content. A personal collection of signatures and phrases has different needs from a support team sharing approved replies. The [shared snippet library article](https://typerelay.com/blog/shared-snippet-library-for-teams-one-source-always-current/) explains how to assign ownership and keep team wording current.

## Connect your everyday tools

Use the web app to organize libraries and manage account settings. Install the [desktop app](../desktop/installation) to search and expand snippets in supported applications. Read [sync and offline use](../guide/sync) to understand local libraries, enrollment and access to content after it has synchronized.

For custom integrations, start with the [REST API](../api/). For compatible AI assistants, follow [MCP setup](../mcp/setup). Access depends on your plan, credential scopes and current content permissions; connecting a tool does not change who can read or edit a library.

## Manage access as your needs change

Keep personal content private and share only the libraries intended for your team. Review [teams and roles](../guide/teams) before inviting members, and use account settings to manage authorized apps and tokens. Account owners and admins manage subscription changes through **Settings → Subscription**.

If you need to operate the infrastructure yourself, use the [self-hosting guide](../selfhosted/) and review the [open-source overview](https://typerelay.com/open-source/). Self-hosting gives you responsibility for deployment, security, email and recovery, while hosted accounts use the managed service and its plan limits.

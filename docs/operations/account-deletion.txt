Account deletion fulfillment

Before enabling this release in production, assign a daily operator for
/admin/account-deletions and confirm the published seven-day processing target.
The scheduler must run AdminAccounts.recover. A request immediately revokes
access; the completion email is sent only after final cleanup succeeds.

Owned accounts use the existing account-purge process. Inspect failed account
purges in /admin, correct external-service failures, and retry there. Personal
cleanup errors also appear in /admin/account-deletions and retry automatically.

Requests with current or historical non-owned account references require review.
Older snippets do not reliably record their author. Do not infer authorship from
library ownership, and do not mark review complete simply because a library is
now absent or inaccessible to the requester.

For each listed account, the authorized support/data operator must inspect its
current and trashed libraries, snippet contents, conflicts, and MigrationBackup
records. Correlate the requester ID/email with available membership, operation,
audit, and backup records. Resolve uncertain contributions with the account's
data owner using the normal privacy-request process; do not require the requester
to contact support, rejoin a former team, or do cleanup themselves. Keep the
request pending if attribution or a lawful retention obligation is unresolved.

Remove identified personal contributions through the existing snippet/library
purge service within an AccountAccess.write transaction, so revisions and sync
notifications remain correct. Remove related conflict copies and migration
backups, including records authored by another person that contain the deleted
content. Use Libraries.cleanupAssets with the affected asset IDs after checking
surviving references. Do not delete unrelated team content or shared assets still
referenced by surviving content. Record any legally required retention and its
basis in the organization's privacy-request record; notify the person of any
exception or delay without requiring them to take another action.

Verify the remaining current content and historical copies no longer contain the
identified personal data. Only then use Record completed personal-data review,
confirming the user ID. That action is audited and releases the final automated
cleanup. Refresh the queue to verify completion or investigate a remaining error.
A pending request is not a completed deletion and must not be reported as one.

DBH captures mail in Mailpit and may disable the scheduler for safety. Tests call
recovery explicitly; do not enable a safety-disabled scheduler just to test this.
